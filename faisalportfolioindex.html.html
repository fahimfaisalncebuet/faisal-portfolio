Selected work.

Click any row to read technical scope and methodology.

Designed to address salinity intrusion in coastal Bangladesh and industrial effluent recovery without grid electricity. Roder Bari pairs low-cost indigenous clay ceramic tubular/planar membranes with biogenic amorphous silica extracted from rice husk ash to engineer surface hydrophobicity and photothermal efficiency.

Scope · Ceramic membrane fabrication · Rice husk silica extraction · Surface modification · Solar thermal desalination

Conventional liquid vaporizers rely on continuous electrical heating and exhibit non-linear evaporation spikes. AuraStone engineers interconnected mesopores and controlled pore tortuosity within a sintered ceramic nanocomposite matrix, governing steady-state spatial release of active repellent molecules strictly via Fick's First Law of Diffusion.

Scope · Mesoporous silica-ceramic matrices · Controlled release kinetics · Pore size distribution · Zero-energy vector control

Investigates green, plant-extract-mediated synthesis pathways for transition metal oxides (ZnO, Co3O4, and NiO) to tailor crystallite morphology, active surface area, and pseudocapacitive charge-transfer kinetics for high-power electrochemical energy storage electrodes.

Scope · Phytosynthesis · SEM & particle size characterization (FRITSCH ANALYSETTE 3 PRO) · Specific capacitance & cyclic stability

Numerical modeling of microstructured Photonic Crystal Fiber (PCF) geometries using the Finite Element Method (FEM) in COMSOL Multiphysics to optimize relative sensitivity, confinement loss, effective mode area, and birefringence for refractive index sensing.

Scope · COMSOL Multiphysics · Wave optics · Finite Element Analysis (FEM) · Refractive index sensing
