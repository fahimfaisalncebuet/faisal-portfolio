About

I'm an undergraduate researcher in Nanomaterials and Ceramic Engineering at BUET, working where functional materials science meets venture building and corporate strategy.

My lab and modeling work covers solar-driven ceramic membrane distillation (Roder Bari), mesoporous ceramic nanocomposites (AuraStone), phytosynthesized metal-oxide supercapacitor electrodes, and photonic crystal fiber sensors.

Beyond research, I co-founded Gryffindor Lab, a creative, web architecture and business development agency. I also serve as Head of the Student Committee & Convener at the Innovation Cauldron Foundation, where I'm organizing the Nano Innovation Ideas Festival 2026, and I drive B2B partnerships as Business Development Executive at AMC.

I'm open to research collaborations in functional ceramics and desalination, creative and software commissions through Gryffindor Lab, and corporate partnership inquiries.
