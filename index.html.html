Roder Bari

Patent-Pending R&D · BUET · 2026

Solar-Powered Ceramic Membrane Distillation — Engineered a low-cost ceramic membrane coated with biogenic rice-husk silica nanoparticles for off-grid water desalination and industrial effluent recovery.
+

Synthesizes amorphous silica from agricultural rice husk ash to modify the surface hydrophobicity and pore morphology of indigenous clay-based tubular and planar ceramic membranes, enabling high salt rejection and thermal efficiency under direct solar irradiation.

Ceramic Membrane Distillation · Rice Husk Silica Nanocoating · Desalination

AuraStone

Functional Ceramics R&D · BUET · 2026

Mesoporous Ceramic Nanocomposite Platform — Solid-state unpowered mosquito repellent diffuser engineered to regulate spatial vapor release via Fickian diffusion kinetics.
+

Tailors interconnected mesopore tortuosity in sintered ceramic matrices to achieve sustained, zero-electricity active diffusion without synthetic liquid aerosol propellants.

Mesoporous Ceramics · Controlled Release · Fick's First Law of Diffusion

Green Metal-Oxide Electrodes

Energy Storage Research · 2025 – 2026

Phytosynthesized Nanostructures for Supercapacitors — Green synthesis and electrochemical evaluation of transition metal oxides (ZnO, Co3O4, NiO) for high-capacitance electrodes.
+

Investigates plant-extract-mediated synthesis pathways to control crystallite size, specific surface area, and faradaic charge transfer resistance in pseudocapacitive electrodes.

Phytosynthesis · Transition Metal Oxides · Supercapacitor Electrodes

PCF Optical Sensor Modeling

COMSOL Multiphysics · 2026

Photonic Crystal Fiber Waveguide Simulation — Finite element method (FEM) modeling of birefringence, confinement loss, and refractive index sensitivity in microstructure optical fibers.
+

Optimizes air-hole lattice pitch and core geometry in COMSOL Multiphysics to maximize sensitivity for chemical and biomedical analyte detection.

COMSOL Multiphysics · Finite Element Modeling · Photonic Crystal Fibers
